# V2 Art Direction

Status: Figma checkpoint for review. This file does not replace `PRD_V2_KINETIC_BLUEPRINT.md` and does not authorize production implementation.

## Visual DNA

- Personality balance: creative developer 40%, playful experiment 40%, premium engineering 20%.
- Composition rule: creative outside, structured inside.
- Light mode uses a mineral off-white field with slate-blue structure.
- Dark mode uses deep midnight and navy surfaces with separately tuned rules, muted text, and Signal Form lighting.
- Electric and ice blue mark interaction, focus, chapter progress, and selected evidence. They are not background decoration.
- Sora Light/SemiBold carries names and chapter titles. Manrope Regular/Bold carries evidence, roles, metadata, and controls.

## Hero

- The name, portrait, and Signal Form share one layered field. The hero must not resolve into a text column beside a visual panel.
- A neutral silhouette labeled `REAL PORTRAIT REQUIRED` remains until a real portrait is supplied.
- Signal Form ribbons and planes may pass around the portrait, but must not obscure the face.
- Identity and scope remain readable before motion runs.
- Quiet navigation contains Work, About, Contact, and an eclipse/aperture theme control.
- The lower chapter cue introduces KAIROS and establishes that the homepage has five primary chapters.

## Signal Form

- One actor built from routed ribbons, modular planes, connection nodes, and one restrained material-light source.
- Hero state: open orbit with unresolved routes.
- KAIROS state: directional spine that suggests review flow without representing project data.
- Avoid spheres, tori, blobs, network globes, particle clouds, and generic full-screen glow.

## KAIROS Chapter

- Chapter number, name, problem, role, and competition context lead.
- The team-built system, personal reliability contribution, model/data context, and human decision boundary remain visually distinct.
- Source access follows the evidence and boundary at the end of the scan. A separate empty source column was rejected in the checkpoint.
- The artifact area stays labeled `REAL KAIROS ARTIFACT PENDING` until a verified capture is available.

## Responsive Direction

- Mobile keeps the semantic order: project, role/context, evidence, boundary, source, media.
- The hero becomes a composed poster instead of a compressed desktop canvas.
- Signal Form uses Lottie or short video only when the result earns its cost; a static poster is the guaranteed fallback.
- Navigation retains a readable current chapter and 44 px interaction targets.
