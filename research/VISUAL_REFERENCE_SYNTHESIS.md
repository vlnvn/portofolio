# Visual reference synthesis

Reviewed 7 September 2026. These references inform principles only; the portfolio does not copy their palette, layout, language, or brand devices.

## Linear

**Useful principle.** Linear's 2026 interface refresh describes a useful hierarchy for dense work: central content stays prominent, orientation controls recede, and structure is conveyed with fewer, softer separators. Its product views also let people choose which properties are visible rather than giving every property equal weight.

**What we will not copy.** No dark product-tool imitation, lavender branding, miniature issue-table styling, command-menu theater, or developer-tool icon language.

**Application.** Keep the compact project register, but reduce the contrast of dates and context. Give roles, evidence, and sources clearer weight. Use thin rules to establish groups and let some boundaries rely on spacing. Dark mode must be designed from semantic tokens rather than produced by inverting the light palette.

Sources: [Linear interface refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), [Linear display options](https://linear.app/docs/display-options).

## Stripe

**Useful principle.** Stripe's interface work shows that depth can belong to the artifact rather than the entire page. Its documented frontend work also treats responsive layout, performance, fallbacks, and reduced-motion behavior as part of the visual result.

**What we will not copy.** No Stripe purple, diagonal gradient fields, fintech illustration language, device mockups, or large conversion copy.

**Application.** Keep text and page structure mostly flat. A real project capture may use one soft shadow and a small radius to separate it from the canvas. Interaction feedback remains short CSS transitions and disappears under reduced motion.

Sources: [Stripe homepage](https://stripe.com/), [Stripe Connect frontend notes](https://stripe.com/blog/connect-front-end-experience).

## Figma

**Useful principle.** Figma combines a neutral framework with occasional concentrated visual moments. Its design-system material treats color modes as token changes, which supports a coherent light/dark implementation instead of scattered component overrides. Real product imagery provides most of the energy.

**What we will not copy.** No oversized color blocks, rainbow palette, playful illustration system, floating cursors, pill-heavy navigation, or imitation of Figma's marketing layout.

**Application.** Let project screenshots retain their natural color. The portfolio supplies neutral surfaces, one teal interaction accent, and one recurring index interaction. The Colors participation result becomes the strongest text-led visual moment because it has no screenshot.

Sources: [Figma](https://www.figma.com/), [Figma design systems and modes](https://www.figma.com/design-systems/).

## Resulting direction

Evidence-first technical work is presented with controlled visual tension and real project artifacts. The page keeps Direction B's fast scan path, but varies rhythm by evidence type: precise for KAIROS, tangible for Ayam Kalintang, text-led for Colors, and compact for supporting experiments. One project-index signal is the recurring visual device. Light and dark modes use the same hierarchy, spacing, and artifact treatment.
