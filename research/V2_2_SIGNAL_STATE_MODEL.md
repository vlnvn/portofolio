# V2.2 Signal Form state model

One assembly persists across `00–06`: `RIBBON_A`, `PLANE_1..3`, and `NODE_1..5`. States change transforms, curvature, spacing, material energy and camera relationship. They never swap meshes or encode literal project data.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00 Hero | Open S-curve, broad depth | Uneven fan | Loose, five stable identities | Medium blue / ice | Near, portrait-aware | Unresolved possibility |
| 01 KAIROS | Directional rising path | Narrow ordered offsets | Ordered along one flow | Cool technical blue | Pulled right beside evidence | Priority and constrained review |

## Continuity rules

- `RIBBON_A`, three planes and five nodes keep stable identities at every state.
- 01→02 and 02→03 interpolate position, rotation, scale and curve control points. No crossfade, teleport, mesh replacement or pop-in.
- SAMBUT reciprocity is conceptual visual language, never a model architecture diagram.
- Reduced motion chooses a deterministic poster for the current semantic chapter.
- Mobile uses poster/video states with the same silhouettes and naming.
