## External asset gates

### Portrait acquisition

- Crop: transparent or clean-background upper torso, approximately chest-up; preserve shoulders.
- Pose: relaxed, mostly forward with a small turn toward the page center; neutral expression.
- Clothing: plain, low-pattern clothing without brand marks.
- Lighting: broad soft key, controlled shadow, neutral white balance; no colored rim light.
- Headroom: at least 12% of source height above hair; keep both shoulders inside the source.
- Silhouette: clean hair and shoulder edges suitable for a real alpha matte.
- Minimum usable resolution: 2400px on the long edge before cutout; avoid upscaled social-media images.
- Light separation: retain a visible dark edge or local contrast against #F3F6FB.
- Dark separation: retain lit cheek/shoulder edges against #09111F without a glow effect.
- Signal Form exclusion: do not cross eyes, nose, mouth or the central face ellipse; overlap may occur behind shoulder/hair only.

### KAIROS artifact acquisition

- State: local replay at pinned commit using a safe fixture; no live service or customer data.
- Required labels: review capacity, queue context, ordered cases, selected/review cutoff and wording that preserves the dispatcher’s decision.
- Safe content: canonical fixture or synthetic-safe repository fixture only; no secrets, customer identifiers or live operational labels.
- Ideal crop: show the queue header, at least several ordered rows and the cutoff together.
- Ideal aspect: test 16:10 first; use 3:2 only if labels remain readable.
- What it proves: a real review interface exists and exposes limited human review capacity.
- Must not imply: deployment, live deliveries, autonomous dispatch, route optimization or production adoption.
- Provenance caption: pinned commit, fixture name, capture date and “local replay”.
